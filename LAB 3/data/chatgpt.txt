ChatGPT is an artificial intelligence chatbot developed by OpenAI. It can understand and generate human-like text, answer questions, explain concepts, help with coding, summarize information, and assist with creative and academic tasks. ChatGPT uses advanced language models to understand the context of a conversation and provide relevant responses. It can be used through a web browser and various applications. Although ChatGPT can be very useful, its responses may sometimes contain mistakes, so important information should be verified from reliable sources.
